---
title: "B-2. Lyrics — Writing Two Versions: One for Display, One for Suno"
---

This chapter explains how the lyrics are made. Writing lyrics is a step the AI drives, and the workflow has no point where it waits for your decision. Still, you can read the finished lyrics, say what bothers you, and have them fixed. If the AI asks "Is this OK?", just answer.

## Flow

1. Based on the plan, the AI decides the song's concept and a hidden backstory that never appears in the lyrics
2. It writes the song's scene as a short story, like a piece of fiction
3. Keeping the world of the story, it cuts out explanations and named emotions, and shapes the text into lyrics
4. Reading aloud, it adjusts syllable counts, rhythm, and rhyme, and finishes the whole song
5. It rereads and revises from three angles: structure, sound, and words. Word counts are checked mechanically
6. It puts together two versions of the lyrics, one for display and one for Suno, and shows them to you

The steps are my own take on lyric-writing techniques that people have long talked about, turned into instructions for the AI.

## If something bothers you, say it in words

When you read the lyrics and something feels off, say it the way you feel it. For RESONAL's Rerun Season, I said this:

> I thought "onaji" / "onnaji" (same) was repetitive. Can you find a more stylish way to say it? (おなじ、おんなじが冗長だと思った。シャレた表現できないかな)

When the AI counted, "onaji" and "onnaji" appeared 6 times in the whole song. So I had it replace lines like "the same billboard, the same rain, the same red light" (おなじ看板 おなじ雨 おなじ赤信号) with words from TV and theater, such as "billboards, rain, red lights, all going by the script" (看板も 雨も 赤信号も 台本どおり).

You can also ask for changes like these.

```
Make the chorus shorter, more decisive
The A section in verse 2 sounds too much like an explanation
Don't use "kimi" (you)
```

## Tell the AI your style preferences during setup

If there are preferences you want kept every time, like "never use this word" or "put a little English in the chorus", tell the AI in the "language and style of the lyrics" question during setup. From the next song on, the AI will follow them. If you want to add more later, just ask the AI and it will write them into the settings.

## Two versions: one for display, one for Suno

The lyrics are written in two versions. One is the display version, in normal Japanese with kanji and kana, used for the subtitles in the music video (MV). The other is the Suno version, which you paste into Suno. In it, kanji are spelled out in hiragana or katakana, because Suno sometimes misreads kanji.

```text:Rerun Season (display version)
交差点の大画面で 私が笑ってる
(On the big screen at the crossing, I'm smiling)
去年の顔のまま ずっと
(With last year's face, all this time)
```

```text:Rerun Season (Suno version)
こうさてんの だいがめんで わたしが わらってる
(On the big screen at the crossing, I'm smiling)
きょねんの かおのまま ずっと
(With last year's face, all this time)
```

The two versions have the same lines and the same content. Only the spelling of the readings changes. When you want to fix the lyrics, ask the AI to fix both the display version and the Suno version.

## Want to know more? Ask the AI

For lyric-writing techniques or what happens inside each step, try asking the AI.

```
Tell me the steps you used to write the lyrics for this song
Explain the techniques used in the lyric-writing skill, for a beginner
```

## Summary

For lyrics, the AI writes a story, cuts it down into lyrics, and rereads and revises them itself. If something bothers you, say it in words and it gets fixed. Preferences you want kept every time can be set during setup. Two versions of the lyrics are written: a display version for the MV subtitles and a Suno version to paste into Suno.
