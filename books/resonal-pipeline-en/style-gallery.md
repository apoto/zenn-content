This chapter shows, with images, the music video (MV) looks included in the public repository. First comes the default template, then the 13 styles in `styles/`. Each image is 4 frames taken from one of RESONAL's published MVs. From the link under each image, you can watch the actual MV moving on YouTube. The character and background art are swapped for each song, so with your own song, both the colors and the art will change.

In the MV step, if you say "I want to choose the look of the MV", the AI suggests candidates based on the song's mood and lyrics. Every style can be used as is or reshaped.

## spectrum-still (default template) — One Still Image × Camera Cuts × Rainbow Light

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-spectrum-still.jpg)
*RESONAL "Killframe" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/bkfLe8vvxvo)*

It shows one still image through camera cuts, with rainbow light behind the subject, a tunnel of light before the chorus, and subtitles that bounce on the beat. This is the look `tools/make_mv.sh` makes out of the box. It suits bright, driving songs with a clear build into the chorus.

## scatter-mincho — Scattered Mincho + Digital Breakdown

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-scatter-mincho.jpg)
*RESONAL "Skip Intro" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/DrbROsdTVws)*

White, extra-bold Mincho text is scattered around the screen, avoiding the center. It features color shifts, sideways streaking afterimages, and static noise that flashes on the hits. It suits dark, aggressive, fast songs.

## ring-visualizer — Neon Circular Visualizer (No Lyrics)

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-ring-visualizer.jpg)
*A sample rendered with the public version's code (material: the cover and audio of RESONAL "Frequency")*

The subject sits in the center, surrounded by neon circular waveforms that wriggle in response to the sound. No lyrics are shown. It suits songs where you want people to listen to the sound more than the words, or instrumentals. RESONAL hasn't used it in a published song, so this one image was rendered as a sample.

## white-cut — White Background × Black Mincho in the Center × Hard Cuts

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-white-cut.jpg)
*RESONAL "Heat Shock" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/LqBNN80fqk4)*

Black Mincho text is placed vertically in the middle of a white background. It hard-cuts on the beat, and leaves "pauses" with nothing on screen between lines. It suits quiet, refined, bittersweet mid-tempo songs.

## photocopy-zine — Zine / Collage Punk

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-photocopy-zine.jpg)
*RESONAL "Repeat Offender" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/5KChsASUCpI)*

Photocopier grain and halftone dots, an off-white and black palette with accents of red-pink, and text that looks cut and pasted. It suits songs that are bright but have some poison in them, and fast.

## pink-bounce — Pink High-Key × Round Letters × Piled-On Decorations

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-pink-bounce.jpg)
*RESONAL "Okawari Apocalypse" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/-ykcryweTcE)*

On a bright, pink-based screen, round extra-bold letters with double outlines bounce in one character at a time. It piles on decorations like hearts and stars. It suits bright, cute songs.

## crimson-gothic — Black × Red Decadent Gothic

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-crimson-gothic.jpg)
*RESONAL "Cold Read" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/Ly2ydNliDqo)*

Based on black and red, Japanese text is shown in Mincho and English in a serif font. It switches by hard cuts between vertical text at the edges, one giant character in the center, and characters stacked one at a time. It suits dark, seductive, ominous songs.

## shape-burst — Bouncing Letters × Bursting Shapes

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-shape-burst.jpg)
*RESONAL "House Arrest" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/1KtxaZI0MoM)*

Extra-bold gothic letters bounce up from below one at a time, and a shape (□○△◇✕) bursts with each letter. It suits cool, provocative songs with a strong groove. With a different color scheme, it also works for bright songs.

## invert-shapes — White Solid Shapes × Negative/Positive Inversion

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-invert-shapes.jpg)
*RESONAL "Static Hazard" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/Zeq87GAhX7Y)*

Extra-bold square gothic text is placed over pure white shapes, and only the parts that overlap the shapes flip to black. It uses just three colors: dark purple, white, and black. It suits dark, aggressive songs.

## sky-keyvisual — One Key Visual × Motion Graphics

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-sky-keyvisual.jpg)
*RESONAL "Extra Time" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/2xJ9oBm7euM)*

It's built in three layers: the background, the character in the center, and white line graphics. At each turn in the song, the density and placement of the graphics change. At key moments, the character sometimes turns into a black silhouette. It suits bright, refreshing, driving songs.

## burn-in — Black Background × Complementary-Color Afterimages

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-burn-in.jpg)
*RESONAL "Afterimage" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/ioRQtF5NO4E)*

White text appears in the center of a pitch-black background, and lyrics that have disappeared stay burned into the same spot as cyan and magenta afterimages. It suits bittersweet, grand songs with a big gap between the quiet parts and the climaxes.

## lowpoly-corridor — Low-Poly 3D World × Moving Camera × Standing Character

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-lowpoly-corridor.jpg)
*RESONAL "Poppy Requiem" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/9u6BlEb3n5E)*

A virtual camera races through a low-poly 3D world, and a transparent character stands inside the 3D space. It suits grand, dark, solemn songs.

## plate-swap — Switching Planes × Still Images × Stamped-In Text

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-plate-swap.jpg)
*RESONAL "Rerun Season" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/_jEE-ct5L8Y)*

A few kinds of backgrounds switch entirely at the start of a bar, and the character is shown by swapping still images. Extra-bold gothic text is stamped in instantly, stays for a while, and disappears in one frame. It suits cool, nighttime-feeling songs at medium to fast tempos.

## portrait-frame — Still Standing Portrait × Switching Backgrounds × Stacked Vertical Text

![](/images/resonal-pipeline/style-portrait-frame.jpg)
*RESONAL "Whiteout" — [Watch the MV on YouTube](https://youtu.be/yUfSKgwiono)*

The standing portrait doesn't move; only the background switches between short videos. Vertical Mincho lyrics stack up from right to left, then disappear all at once. It suits quiet, grand, lyrical songs.

## Summary

The public version includes the default template and 13 styles. Each one is used with your song's colors and art swapped in. Use the ones you like as they are, and if they feel like not enough, you can reshape them or add new ones with the same format, as described in the ["B-5. Music Video (MV)"](https://zenn.dev/apoto/books/resonal-pipeline-en/viewer/music-video) chapter.
