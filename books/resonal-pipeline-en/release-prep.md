---
title: "B-6. Release Prep — Gathering the Assets and Text"
---

This chapter covers the step after you approve the music video (MV): gathering the assets and text you will use to publish. The AI prepares the assets and text. You do the uploading by hand, following a checklist.

## What the AI prepares

What gets made depends on the release destinations you chose during setup. If you chose "don't release," nothing is made.

| Release destination | What the AI prepares |
|---|---|
| YouTube | Title, description, and tag text, plus a thumbnail |
| Streaming services (Spotify, etc.) | Song info such as the title and genre, plus 3000×3000 cover art |
| Other | Cover art |

For every destination, the AI also makes a checklist of the upload steps. The description includes an introduction to the song, the credits, the lyrics, and the generative AI tools used.

The cover art is cropped to a square from the cover image, so the edges may get cut off or fine details may look rough. When that happens, the AI asks whether to "use it as is" or "redraw it as a square."

## If something bothers you, say it in words

The AI shows you the text. If anything bothers you, tell it.

```
Make the start of the description shorter
Add "city pop" to the tags
```

## Check the rules for credits and AI disclosure

How to write the lyricist and composer credits, and how to disclose that you used AI, differ between streaming services and YouTube, and the rules can change. The AI won't tell you a definite way to write them. It will tell you to check. Before uploading, check the latest rules of the services you use.

## You do the uploading

You upload and publish by hand, using your own accounts. The AI goes through the checklist with you from the top, tells you which files to use, and that's it.

## Want to know more? Ask the AI

If you want to know what's in the files it made, or what a checklist item means, ask the AI.

```
Explain each of the files in the release folder
Tell me the steps for uploading to YouTube in a bit more detail
```

## Summary

In release prep, the AI gathers the text and assets, such as cover art, for each release destination you chose. For credits and AI disclosure, check each service's rules. You upload and publish with your own accounts, following the checklist.
