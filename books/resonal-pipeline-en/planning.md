---
title: "B-1. Planning — Pick One of 2–3 Ideas"
---

This chapter explains how planning a new song works. Planning is the first of the 6 steps, and also the first point where you decide.

## Flow

1. Say "I want to make a new song"
2. The AI asks just one question: "Is there anything you want to try this time?"
3. The AI offers 2–3 ideas and stops
4. You pick one
5. The AI writes the plan (`BRIEF.md`)

## One question, then ideas

Before offering ideas, the AI asks only this one question.

> Is there anything you want to try this time (theme, musical style, lyric language, a new challenge, etc.)? If not, "up to you" is fine

If there's nothing in particular, you can go with "up to you." After it gets your answer, the AI reads the kind of music and the world you gave during setup, and offers 2–3 ideas. Each idea lists a working title, a one-line description of the song, the genre and tempo, and the angle for the lyrics.

The ideas are made so they don't come out too similar to songs you've already made. The AI keeps a list of your songs automatically.

## You pick the idea

The AI doesn't narrow it down to one. It lays the ideas side by side, asks "Which one?", and stops. With only one idea, you can only say good or bad. With 2–3, you can compare and choose. The AI never picks one itself and moves on.

When you choose, you don't have to take an idea as is. You can add requests.

```
I'll go with B. But make the tempo as laid-back as A
Mix A and C. I like C's title better
None of these click. Give me brighter ideas
```

The idea you choose is written up in the plan, `BRIEF.md`. Lyrics, composing, and the music video (MV) all build on this plan. The ideas you didn't choose stay in the plan as candidates.

## Want to know more? Ask the AI

To learn how the plan is written, or the rules for coming up with ideas, ask the AI.

```
Tell me what each item in BRIEF.md is used for
What rules do you follow when you come up with ideas?
```

## Summary

In planning, the AI asks one question, then offers 2–3 ideas, and you pick one. When you pick, you can add requests or mix ideas. The idea you pick is written up in the plan, which the later steps build on.
