Thank you for reading this far.

In this book, I explained the public version of the workflow, which turns RESONAL's production setup into something anyone can use, from setup through the six steps. Looking back, I think the heart of the workflow is these three things:

- A machine tells you what to do next: `next.sh` looks at what's in the song folder and returns just one item, so neither people nor the AI need to remember the order
- A machine decides when a step is done: It checks whether a step is finished not only by whether a file exists, but by measuring what's inside, such as loudness and image size
- People focus on decisions and words: People decide in four places (choosing an idea, generating the audio, watching the music video (MV), and uploading), and name what's missing when they give feedback

The AI makes the first draft, and people check it, decide, and give direction to make it better. The AI is a tool that does the hands-on work, and I see the making itself as an extension of human creativity.

## If you'd like to try it

The public version of the workflow is on GitHub. Just open Claude Code, paste the URL, and ask like this to get started. How to ask when using another AI agent is in the ["Introduction"](https://zenn.dev/apoto/books/resonal-pipeline-en/viewer/start-here#using-an-agent-other-than-claude-code) chapter.

```
https://github.com/apoto/resonal-workflow
I want to make songs together with AI using the workflow at this link.
Please bring it onto my computer and start with the setup. Ask me if anything is unclear.
If you can't work with files on my computer, tell me how to get started with Claude Code instead.
```

After that, it will ask you in conversation about the kind of music you want to make, its world, your character, and the services you'll use. When setup is done, say "I want to make a new song," and your first song begins.

https://github.com/apoto/resonal-workflow

## As v1

This book and the public version of the workflow capture their form as of October 2026 as "v1." Models have improved a lot in just the past few months, and I keep hearing about new models in video generation too. I'll keep changing RESONAL's own workflow to keep up with those changes, but the public version will stay in this form. I may fix bugs that stop it from working at all.
