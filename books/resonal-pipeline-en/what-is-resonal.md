## Why I started

I had been interested in composing for a long time, and I bought a DAW and sound libraries to learn. But after my child was born, it became hard to find a solid block of time to open the DAW.

Around then, I saw how much music AI had improved. I thought I might be able to make work of a decent quality even in spare moments. That's how it started.

## Why I made the workflow public

When I exhibited RESONAL at [Generative AI Anything Expo Vol.6](https://www.genai-expo.com/), I got a lot of questions like "How do you make the videos?" and "Is the mastering automatic too?" So I decided to shape our production steps into something anyone can use, and make it public.

## How we work

This is the setup we use to make RESONAL.

- mac mini: always on, running production and automated tasks from planning to release
- MacBook: used now and then, for things like manual mastering and testing
- Phone: where I check the results and send back instructions (I receive them in Slack and reply in the Claude app)

The two computers share the repository through GitHub. Large files like audio (WAV) are handled with Git LFS.

I usually don't sit at my computer. I check progress and results on my phone. So every result is sent to Slack automatically as soon as it's finished.
